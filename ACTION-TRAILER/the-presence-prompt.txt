==============================================================
THE PRESENCE — 12-SECOND CINEMATIC SHOT
Cinema Studio 3.0 · Seedance 2.0 · "Option A"
==============================================================

CONCEPT
-------
No VFX. No transformation. Pure cinema.
A man standing still while the world moves.
The camera draws closer. He acknowledges nothing.
Then — one gesture. The shades come off. Eyes revealed.
That is the entire film.

CHARACTER DESCRIPTION (for @ref1 — character sheet)
----------------------------------------------------
Broad, powerfully built Black man. Deep ebony complexion — rich, even
dark brown skin tone. Very wide shoulders, thick neck, stocky muscular
frame. Broad facial structure: wide forehead, prominent high cheekbones,
strong square jaw. Full thick black beard covering entire jaw and chin,
dense mustache connecting. Short natural coil/afro-texture hair — tight
curl pattern, low skin fade on sides, slightly fuller rounded top.
Heavy-lidded dark brown eyes — calm, intense, unhurried.

OUTFIT (for start frame generation)
------------------------------------
All-black premium fitted outfit. Black high-collar jacket or premium
black coat — structured, sharp, minimal. No logos, no detail. The
darkness of the clothing pushes all attention to the face and presence.
Dark aviator-style sunglasses — matte black frames, dark lenses.
The shades are on. Eyes hidden.

ENVIRONMENT
-----------
High rooftop terrace at late golden hour — sky transitioning from
amber below to deep blue-grey above. City stretching to horizon.
Rain-slicked concrete surface reflecting warm city light.
Wind is present — subtle movement in clothing. Nothing theatrical.
The light: warm amber key from city glow below catching jaw and
cheekbones from beneath, cool blue-grey fill from the overcast sky
above. The intersection of both lights on deep ebony skin creates
extraordinary dimensional contrast. This is the money lighting setup.

Film grain. Cinematic. This man costs the camera nothing.

Style & Mood: ARRI Alexa 35 cinematography. 50mm anamorphic lens.
Teal + orange color grade — warm amber on deep ebony skin, cool

genre. This is pure presence filmmaking — no VFX, no gimmicks.
The camera is the only thing that moves. Christopher Nolan meets

still that says nothing and means everything.

Narrative Summary: A man stands alone above the city — still,
unreadable, complete. The world moves around him. He moves for
nothing. Then he removes his sunglasses and the world finally
sees who it has been dealing with.

Dynamic Description:

SHOT 1 [00:00–00:03] WIDE ESTABLISHING. @ref1 stands on the rain-slicked
rooftop terrace. Medium shot, slightly low angle — city stretching
behind and below. He is absolutely still. Wind moves clothing
slightly. Rain-slicked surface beneath. Camera begins an
imperceptible slow push-in — barely moving but always moving.
Speed: Slow-mo. The world behind him is alive. He is not.
He is the still point in a turning world.

SHOT 2 [00:03–00:05] TIGHTER PUSH. Camera has closed ground — now a
medium close-up. His broad face fills more of the frame.
The dark aviator shades are fully visible — matte black, impenetrable.
The amber city light catching across jaw and cheekbones below the
lenses. The shadows where his eyes should be are completely black.
The audience still cannot read him. Camera continues pushing.
Slow-mo sustained.

SHOT 3 [00:05–00:09] THE REMOVAL. Bullet Time engages.
One hand rises — slow, deliberate, unhurried. No urgency.
He is doing this on his schedule, not ours. The sunglasses
are lifted from his face in extreme slow motion. The moment
the lenses clear his eyes — Bullet Time at maximum.
His dark brown eyes are revealed. Heavy-lidded. Calm. Looking
slightly toward the horizon — not at the camera. As if whatever
is out there is already handled. Amber light now catches across
the bridge of his nose and upper cheeks. The eyes are extraordinary —
deep, warm, and completely still. This is the climax.

SHOT 4 [00:09–00:11] ECU — EYES ONLY. Extreme close-up.
His eyes. The full weight of the shot lands here.
No movement. Camera locked. Just the eyes and the world
they are looking at. The grain of his skin visible.
The warm light. The blue shadow. Hold.

SHOT 5 [00:11–00:12] PULL BACK — WIDE. A single slow breath
of camera — pulling back just slightly. Returning to wide.
He is still there. Still still. The city still moving behind him.
Nothing has changed. And yet everything has changed.
Hard cut to black.

Static Description: High urban rooftop terrace. Rain-slicked
concrete. No railing visible — edge of the world. City lights
extending to horizon below. Sky: amber near the horizon line,
deep blue-grey overhead, transitioning through the frame.
Subtle wind. No other people. The location is a throne.

Audio: Single low piano note from 00:00. Barely audible.
Cello enters at 00:05 as shades begin to move — single low
sustained note, no melody. At 00:09 when eyes are revealed:
near silence. Only ambient city sound far below. Wind.
Rain on concrete. His breathing. Hold through to black.
