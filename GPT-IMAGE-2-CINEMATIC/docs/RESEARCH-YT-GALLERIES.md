## 3. Typography-in-Image Winning Patterns

1. **Movie Poster Bold Serif** — Large serif headline (Trajan, Didot, Caslon) centered top-third, all-caps, color-pop on dark background + supporting sans-serif subtitle below
2. **Magazine Masthead + Coverline** — Logo masthead top-left in serif or custom script, large coverline (40-80pt) positioned right-third or center with pull-quote style, smaller byline/date strip
3. **Brutalist Sans All-Caps** — Helvetica or Futura bold condensed, stark geometric alignment, minimal spacing, 1-3 words max, high contrast white-on-black or reversed
4. **Neon Holographic Text** — Glowing sci-fi font with lens flare, floating above scene, color-shifted magenta/cyan, shallow DOF blur on background, synthetic glow
5. **Newspaper Headline Old-Timey** — Serif body font dense text block, column layout, actual news-style hierarchy, dirty paper texture, vintage halftone quality
6. **Luxury Serif Elegant** — High-fashion serif (Caslon, Sabon, Gotham Cond) light-weight, kerned precise, positioned negative space, whisper-quiet color + metallic accents
7. **Hand-Lettered Rough Editorial** — Calligraphic or semi-rough brush stroke style, expressive organic form, positioned off-grid, color matches illustration mood
8. **Technical Blueprint Font** — Monospace OCR or technical font, small dense text layout, technical specs or schematic style, technical cyan on black or blueprint paper

## 4. Camera & Lens Recipes Most Cited

### Primary Focal Lengths & Framing
- **21mm Ultra-Wide** → epic landscape, architectural, world-building, "seeing the whole stage"
- **35mm Standard** → neutral narrative frame, natural human POV, editorial/documentary
- **50mm Dramatic** → intimate close-up, portrait, psychological intensity, "human eye equivalent"
- **85mm Intimate** → shallow DOF portraiture, isolation of subject, emotional focus (f/1.4–f/2 typical)
- **135mm Compression** → distant subject feeling close, telephoto compression, landscape intimacy

### Depth of Field Combos
- **f/1.2–f/1.8 Razor-Thin** → single eyelash sharp, emotional isolation, luxury/fashion, bokeh character
- **f/2.8–f/4 Cinematic Mid** → subject isolated, background design-conscious, "movie frame" feel
- **f/8–f/11 Deep Focus** → architectural, wide vistas, intentional sharp layers, prestige/control

### Camera Format Language
- **"35mm film aesthetic, Kodak Portra 400"** → warm grain structure, color rendition accuracy, vintage feel
- **"Shot on ARRI Alexa 65, anamorphic 1.85:1"** → premium film look, lens flare bokeh, theatrical prestige
- **"Panavision Primo 70mm anamorphic"** → ultra-cinematic stretch, dramatic bokeh, legendary reference
- **"DCI 4K digital, graded 2.39:1 scope"** → modern theatrical, wide-format narrative, contemporary premium

## Key Learnings From GPT Image 2 Era (Apr 2026)

- **Text Accuracy Above 95%** across Latin, CJK, Arabic scripts — enables true magazine covers, billboards, newspaper scenes, packaging with readable tiny text
- **Film Stock Language Works Best** — "Kodak Portra 400 aesthetic" more reliable than "8K masterpiece"; grain structure responds stock-specifically
- **Avoid Keyword Spam** — "trending on artstation, 8K, cinematic, masterpiece" returns generic AI look; use director language instead
- **Lighting Must Be Directional** — "harsh cyan rim light from left, soft tungsten key from right" beats "cinematic lighting"; vectors matter
- **Negative Prompt Power** — Specify *what not to do*: "no plastic sheen, no smoothed skin, no digital artifacts" constrains output better than listing what to include
- **Typography Placement Requires Specificity** — "top-left bold sans, middle-right serif subline" with exact % position or grid reference works; vague "add text somewhere" fails
